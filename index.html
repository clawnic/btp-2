Executive summary

Conventional MPPT (P&O) collapses under partial shading because it locks onto the nearest local peak. Metaheuristics (PSO, vanilla FOA) reach the global peak but at the cost of slow convergence, large steady-state oscillation, and high run-to-run variance. AH-FOA closes all three gaps simultaneously and delivers the most consistent tracking across uniform, light, heavy and severe shading conditions.

01 · Problem

Partial Shading & Localized Heating

🌥️

Multi-peak P-V curve

Bypass diodes around shaded modules introduce multiple local maxima. Hill-climbing algorithms like P&O latch onto the first peak they see and mistake it for the optimum, leaving 25–46 % of available power on the table.

🔥

Hotspot formation

Stuck on a local peak, shaded cells are reverse-biased and dissipate the string current as heat. Sustained operation on an LMPP accelerates panel ageing and can cause permanent damage. Fast global tracking is therefore both an efficiency and a reliability issue.

03 · Algorithms

The contenders

P&O

Perturb & Observe

Hill-climbing on dP/dV. Trivially cheap, deterministic, but partial-shading-blind.

Femia et al., IEEE Trans. PE 2005
PSO

Particle Swarm Optim.

Population-based; pbest+gbest velocity update. Reaches GMPP but high variance and oscillation.

Ishaque et al., IEEE Trans. IE 2012
FOA

Falcon Optimization

Three-phase falcon hunt: search → spiral → dive. Deterministic uniform init.

Alshareef, IEEE Access 2022
PROPOSED
AH-FOA

Adaptive Hybrid FOA

FOA + adaptive AP/DP + Lévy flights + P&O hand-off + elitist warm restart.

07 · Takeaways

What this work claims

🎯

Robustness

AH-FOA is the only algorithm that stays above 90 % efficiency across every shading condition we tested.

📉

Stability

Steady-state ripple drops from 30–40 W (vanilla FOA/PSO) to ~0 W thanks to the P&O hand-off — protecting the converter from thermal stress.

🔁

Reproducibility

Run-to-run standard deviation roughly halves vs vanilla FOA, making thesis figures reliable across seeds.
